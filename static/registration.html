RULES & REGULATIONS

  1. Please submit your photo ID and address proof when joining the PG.
  2. Management is not responsible for residents' personal belongings. Keep valuables such as mobile phones, cash, gold, and laptops safely secured.
  3. Give management at least 15 days' notice before leaving the hostel; otherwise, the advance will not be refunded.
  4. Rent is due on or before the 5th of each calendar month. Late payment may attract a penalty based on the number of calendar days late.
  5. All visitors must enter their name, address, and mobile number in the register. Visitors are not allowed after 12:00 a.m. Residents working night shifts must inform management in advance.
  6. Token advance rent is non-refundable once paid.
  7. Inform management in advance about guest arrivals. Guest accommodation costs INR 500 per day, with or without food.
  8. Use dustbins and keep your surroundings neat and clean.
  9. Liquor consumption and smoking are strictly prohibited inside rooms and the PG.
  10. Iron boxes are not allowed inside the PG. Submit your iron box to management before joining and collect it when vacating.
  11. Please avoid wasting electricity, food, and water.
  12. Maintain silence in and around the PG, as families live nearby.
  13. Management may inspect rooms through wardens or supervisors at any time.
  14. If misconduct is observed, management may ask you to vacate the PG within five days.
  15. A maintenance charge of INR 1,000 will be collected when vacating the PG.
  16. CCTV cameras are used to monitor the PG premises.
  17. Close the main gates and doors properly, especially when returning during night shifts.
  18. Do not mistreat other residents. Management may take appropriate action if this rule is violated.
  19. Return your room keys to the key board when leaving. Charges may apply for lost keys.

Your details and photos are stored encrypted. Room assignment and check-in are confirmed by the owner after approval.
